Map loop draws your run as a closed loop in verified-empty ocean, sized so the loop equals your distance — purely so Strava shows a map. Near Watopia (12°S 166°E) parks it in the same South Pacific neighbourhood where Zwift georeferences Watopia, 80+ km clear of any land, road or Zwift island. Point Nemo (49°S 123°W) is the oceanic pole of inaccessibility — 2,700 km from the nearest land, where retired spacecraft are deorbited. Pick no map for a plain activity. Either way the track can’t touch real-world segments or leaderboards.
